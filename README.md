#
Test branch of DCSS: Kimchi. Codename - Spicy

Kimchi is my favorite branch of O.G Crawl including the old thematic parts of it that I loved that the latest versions/trunks removed, with an emphasis on (un)balanced expansion in favor of player power/fun and chaotic variability. From a design standpoint I wanted to create this branch and use it as an opportunity to learn more about one of my favorite games, while also updating it to include some of the modern new changes.

New End Supergoal:
-Because of considerable increased length, access to loot, drops, and increased power due to unbalanced classes/builds in this version, I want there to be two additional 'challenge' runs, both essentially the same. Orb of Zig1 and Zig2. Essentially, you need to go down, grab the orb, and take it to Zig27, annoit it there, and then escape. Doing it once gives you Zig1, twice (with the miniature found at the end of the first zig) gives Zig2. Escpaing with one of those orbs then enshrines your character as a new named unique, who then always spawns on Zig27 along with everything else.
